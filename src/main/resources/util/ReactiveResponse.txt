1) difference b/w Mono<ResponseEntity<T> v/s ResponseEntity<Mono<T>> :
sol :

-> preferred pattern for "I will asynchronously produce the entire ResponseEntity"
		database call
		     ↓
		Mono<User>
		     ↓
		decide: 200 or 404
		     ↓
		ResponseEntity<User>
		     ↓
		Mono<ResponseEntity<User>> 
		
-> prefferred pattern for "The HTTP response/status/headers are available immediately, but the body will be produced asynchronously"
		ResponseEntity
		 ├── status: 200
		 ├── headers: ...
		 └── body: Mono<User>
		              ↓
		          async result
